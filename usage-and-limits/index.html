Usage and limits

Your plan sets two limits, which we share with you along with your key:

  • a rate limit, in requests per minute
  • a quota, in requests per billing period

What counts toward your quota​

Every successful (200) response counts toward your quota. Errors don't.

When you hit a limit​

LimitResponseWhat to do
Rate limit429, with a Retry-After headerWait that many seconds, then retry
Quota403, with a message saying when the quota resetsWait for the reset, or contact us to raise it
